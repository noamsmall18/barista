Marketbar 1.2.0 adds a risk-adjusted view of your portfolios, prices the overnight
session, and fixes the menu-bar dropdown misbehaving.

**Download `Marketbar-1.2.0.zip`**, unzip it and drag `Marketbar.app` to
Applications. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13 or later.
It isn't notarized, so macOS will block the first launch. Open it once, then go
to System Settings -> Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. Or
[build it from source](https://github.com/noamsmall18/barista#install), which
avoids the prompt entirely.

## New

- **Sharpe ratio per portfolio.** Annualized from daily closes, net of an
  editable risk-free rate (4% by default), shown as a table in the Marketbar
  window and in the dropdown. It needs five daily returns and is flagged as
  early below twenty.
- **Overnight prices.** Between the 8 PM after-hours close and pre-market, the
  menu bar shows the overnight session's trades (marked "ON") from Robinhood's
  public 24/5 quotes, instead of freezing on the 4 PM close.
- **All-time range.** Each ticker's Overview shows how far it is from its
  all-time high and low.
- **Portfolio web dashboard** served locally by the app, plus analyst
  expectations in research.
- **Portfolio history** recorded at one-minute resolution, with backup recovery.
- **Realized YTD checkpoint.** Enter the year-to-date realized total your broker
  reports, and Marketbar keeps it without touching your trades, shares, cost
  basis or cash.
- **Research runs on its own**, independent of the menu-bar app.
- A tidier portfolio page with stat tiles, and a new Marketbar icon.

## Fixes

- **Edits made in the dropdown now show in the menu bar right away.** They used
  to wait for the next refresh, up to five minutes after the close.
- **Clicking the menu-bar item no longer closes the dropdown and immediately
  reopens it.**
- **A new day's gain starts at midnight ET** after the last session. Overnight
  and early pre-market no longer count the previous day's move a second time.
- Stock chart tooltips no longer overlap, and charts no longer flicker on refresh.
- Launching Marketbar twice no longer starts a second copy.
