# Marketbar research desk rebuild

The requested outcome is a substantially different research product, not another
round of dashboard decoration. The existing calculations and data-source limits
remain accurate while the interaction model and presentation change.

## Completion requirements

1. Replace the long scrolling dashboard with five navigable workspaces. Today
   starts with research priorities. Companies provides a persistent universe,
   a company dossier, and a research notebook alongside the evidence. Compare,
   Portfolio, and Theses each serve a separate task.
2. Replace the visual system: compact terminal chrome, editorial typography,
   a warm paper research surface, a dark market rail, and purposeful split panes.
   All workspaces must work at desktop and narrow widths with keyboard access.
3. Make research actionable: per-security stages, conviction, review date,
   evidence categorized as support/challenge/question, and completable questions.
   Save these on the Mac, retain previous notes, and preserve new drafts during
   saves and symbol changes. Surface this work in Today and the thesis board.
4. Add an editable EPS × multiple valuation lab with bear/base/bull assumptions,
   a sensitivity matrix, and explicit source and currency limitations. Persist
   assumptions and export a complete company memo.
5. Build a comparison lab combining price history with cross-company reported
   metrics. Preserve fiscal-period, currency, coverage, and missing-data context.
6. Verify native storage validation and old-note compatibility, meaningful web
   workflows, rendered desktop/mobile screens, and a signed release app. Do not
   claim full XCTest execution if the local toolchain cannot run it.

No external packages, remote fonts, fabricated live data, or ledger writes.
